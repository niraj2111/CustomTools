ABOUT

A tool for thinking through generative art.

Generative art begins with a system, but the work does not end there. The same rules can produce many forms; attention and choice give one of them meaning.

Here, you move between chance and intention—randomising, adjusting, locking, rejecting and keeping. Authorship gathers across those decisions rather than sitting only in the code or final image.

The vase becomes a portrait without likeness. No parameter has a fixed meaning. Character emerges through your association with posture, proportion and presence.

The final plotted drawing carries the whole process: a shared system, its variations, and the form you chose to recognise.
