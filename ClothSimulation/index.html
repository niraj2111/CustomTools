Interactive canvas

Cloth Studio

Shape a masked fabric, pull it into place, or cut new openings.

Preparing cloth… Controls that change the cloth or masks rebuild it automatically.

On the canvas Drag a point to pull the cloth. Choose Tear to cut; a click makes a small cut. Hold C for a temporary tear brush.

Rebuilding Shape, mask, and anchor changes create a fresh cloth, so manual drags and cuts are cleared. Motion and appearance controls update the current cloth.

Shortcuts R rebuilds; S exports SVG. The mask shape controls are shared by both mask layers.
