Notes:
  • This tool follows the same spiral construction style as your standalone stone sketch.
  • Norm Spiral uses direct x = w * theta * cos(theta * freq) growth with separate noise channels.
  • Split Spiral uses the three-band y split logic with independent top and bottom offsets.
  • Stretch lets you click-drag to draw multiple guide lines. Click a line to make it active, then drag an endpoint to edit it.
  • Noise Oct and Noise Fall map directly to noiseDetail(...).
  • Zoom and Fit View only affect the preview.
