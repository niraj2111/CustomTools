fabric

Thread-mesh textile generator with independent outer-band distortion, paper-sized preview, and SVG export.

Notes:
  • Mesh distortion and outer-band distortion are driven by separate seeded noise fields.
  • Preview Curves smooths the on-screen and SVG paths using Catmull-Rom splines.
  • Randomize Fields changes both distortion offsets without changing your control values.
  • Zoom and Fit View only affect the preview.
